How to read these wireframes

These are screen-layout recommendations. Only the four navigation buttons and this notes dialog are interactive. Choice panels and action labels illustrate the proposed game; they do not submit actions or modify your saved incident.

  • The immediate problem and choices receive most of the screen. The scene supplies relevant spatial context.
  • The opening removes the separate briefing and controls tour. Three qualified approaches make the first decision accessible.
  • Allocation introduces inventory only when the player uses it. Exact needs remain beside the controls.
  • The outcome leads with consequences and a named replay invitation. Detailed reports become optional.
  • Shown times assume a proposed 07:00 opening. The new engine must calculate actual readiness and preserve check-in before work.
  • Default screen fit is a design target. Large text and zoom must retain vertical reflow rather than clipping content.
