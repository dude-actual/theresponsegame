THE RESPONSE GAME

What happens next depends on how you respond.

The Response Game began with a simple idea:

What if the experience didn't follow a predetermined path?

You respond to what's in front of you.

Your response changes what comes next.
And then you respond again.

A dynamic experience built around realistic communication, uncertainty, decision-making, coordination, competing priorities, consequences, and an environment that evolves based on the choices people make.

No two paths have to be exactly the same.

TheResponseGame.com is available for development, partnership, or acquisition.
